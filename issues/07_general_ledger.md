# Issue: General Ledger & Reconciliation

**Goal**: Unified ledger across all units with reconciliation and audit trail.

## Description
- Combined ledger view across Kantor, Kantin, Koperasi.
- Filter by date, unit, category, payment method.
- Fast search.
- Daily reconciliation by Kepala Keuangan.
- Audit trail (who, when, what changed).

## Acceptance Criteria
- Ledger shows all transactions from all units.
- Filters and search work correctly.
- Reconciliation workflow is functional.
- Audit trail captures all changes.
